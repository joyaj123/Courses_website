Vectorization and Loop Unrolling

Loop unrolling is a space-time tradeoff. By duplicating the loop's body and reducing the iteration count, we minimize branch prediction misses. However, aggressive unrolling can lead to instruction cache pressure.

The Impact on L1 Cache

Data locality is the single most important factor in modern performance. When your compiler rearranges data structures to fit neatly into 64-byte cache lines, you avoid the "Memory Wall"—the massive latency gap between CPU cycles and DRAM access.
